Welcome to Penelope's

Tucked away on Stour Street in the heart of Manningtree, Penelope's is the warm, welcoming spot for a fresh breakfast, a proper sandwich, a slice of homemade cake and a really good cup of coffee — and a full afternoon tea if you book ahead. Pop in, take a seat, and let us look after you.

Questions & Answers

Do I need to book?

Not for breakfast, sandwiches or cake — just pop in. Afternoon tea is bookings only, so please ring us on 01206 970654.

Do you make your own cakes?

We do! Our homemade cakes and bakes are made fresh, and the counter changes through the week.

Can I take food away?

Yes — you can eat in or take away. We don't do delivery.

Do you do special orders or catering?

Give us a ring on 01206 970654 or message us on social media to ask about occasion cakes and larger orders.

What are your opening hours?

Monday 9:00am – 2:30pm, Tuesday to Friday 9:00am – 4:00pm, Saturday 8:30am – 3:00pm. Closed Sunday.

Where are you?

We're at 21 Stour Street, Manningtree, Essex, CO11 1DH — right in the heart of town.
