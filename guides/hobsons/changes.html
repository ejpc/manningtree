Changing your own prices and text

If a price goes up or your hours change, you can update it yourself in about a minute. You don't need to know anything technical — you're just typing over some words.

You cannot permanently break this.

Every previous version of your website is saved automatically, forever. If something goes wrong, ring or email me and I'll put it back exactly as it was in about a minute. So don't be nervous about trying.

The only rule

Your website is made of ordinary words mixed in with instructions for the computer. The computer's instructions live inside pointy brackets, like this: <h3>

Change the ordinary words. Never touch anything inside pointy brackets. That's the whole rule.

Here's a real line from your prices page. The grey parts are the computer's; the highlighted parts are yours to change:

<span class="n"><b>Beard Trim</b>
<span class="d">shaped and tidied</span></span>
<span class="p">£7</span>

So to put the beard trim up to £8, you change £7 to £8 and leave every bracket exactly as it is.

How to do it

  1. Create a free GitHub account Only needed once. Go to github.com, sign up, then send me your username and I'll give you access to your own website. Skip this if I've already done it.
  2. Open the link I sent you It goes straight to the right page. Bookmark it — it's the same link every time.
  3. Click the pencil icon Top right of the page, above the text. That switches it into edit mode.
  4. Find the bit you want to change Press Ctrl and F together (or Cmd and F on a Mac), then type what you're looking for — a price, or a word. It'll jump straight to it.
  5. Type over it Change only the words or numbers. Leave the pointy brackets alone.
  6. Click the green "Commit changes" button Top right. A box appears — just click the green button in that too.
  7. Wait a minute, then check your website It takes about sixty seconds to update. Refresh the page and your change will be there.
⚠ Things to leave to me

Adding photos, changing colours, adding a whole new section, or anything to do with the map or the booking button. Those aren't dangerous, they're just fiddlier than they look. Email me and I'll do it — small tweaks are usually free, bigger jobs are £15.

Honestly, though

Plenty of people read this, decide life's too short, and just email me the change instead. That's completely fine and I'm not offended. The option is there if you want it, and if you don't, one email from you does the same job.
