Welcome to Rileys

Right on Manningtree High Street, Rileys is the local barbershop for a sharp, well-finished cut. From precision scissor work and skin fades to clean beard trims and the kids' first chair, our barbers take the time to get it right — in a proper old-school shop of brick, mahogany and leather chairs. Book online in seconds or pop in and take a seat.

Questions & Answers

Do I need to book?

You can book online through our Fresha page for a time that suits you, or pop in and we'll fit you in when we can.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes — we cut boys aged 5 to 10, and there's a kids' price of £16.

Is there a senior citizen price?

Yes, there's a special price of £16 for over 67s.

Do you do beard trims?

We do — beard trims start from £7, shaped and tidied to suit you.

What are your opening hours?

Tuesday to Thursday 8:30am – 5:30pm, Friday 8:30am – 6:00pm, Saturday 8:30am – 4:30pm. Closed Sunday and Monday.

Where are you?
